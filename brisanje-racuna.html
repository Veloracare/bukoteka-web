Brisanje računa i podataka.

Račun salona u Bukoteci i sve njegove podatke možeš obrisati sam u aplikaciji ili zatražiti brisanje e-poštom.

U aplikaciji

  1. Prijavi se u Bukoteku računom salona (na webu ili u aplikaciji za Android ili iOS).
  2. Otvori Postavke i najprije odaberi Preuzmi sigurnosnu kopiju ako želiš sačuvati podatke.
  3. Na dnu Postavki odaberi Obriši račun i podatke.
  4. Za potvrdu upiši OBRIŠI ili točan naziv salona i potvrdi trajno brisanje.

E-poštom

Pošalji zahtjev s adrese kojom se prijavljuješ na advisory@veloracare.io s predmetom „Bukoteka – brisanje računa” i nazivom salona. Zahtjev rješavamo bez nepotrebnog odgađanja, najkasnije u roku od mjesec dana.

Što se briše

Raspored i termini, klijenti i telefoni, usluge i radno vrijeme, neradni dani, spremljena ranija stanja, online postavke, poveznica i rezervacije, povratne informacije, veza s prijavom i zapis o pretplati u Bukoteci. Brisanje je trajno i ne može se poništiti.

Što se zadržava

Samo tehnički zapis o iskorištenoj probi: interna oznaka računa te početak i kraj tridesetodnevne probe, bez e-maila, naziva salona i podataka klijenata. Tako se besplatna proba ne može ponovno pokrenuti brisanjem i ponovnim otvaranjem računa. Kratkotrajni zapisi zaštite od zloupotrebe (hash IP adrese) sami se uklanjaju nakon približno 24 sata.

Zapis prijave u Firebase Authentication (Google, Apple ili e-mail) i eventualni zapis kupca u Stripeu uklanjaju se u roku od 30 dana od brisanja računa.

Delete your account and data.

You can delete your Bukoteka business account and all of its data yourself in the app, or request deletion by e-mail.

In the app

  1. Sign in to Bukoteka with your business account (on the web or in the Android or iOS app).
  2. Open Settings and first choose Download backup if you want to keep your data.
  3. At the bottom of Settings choose Delete account and data.
  4. To confirm, type DELETE (or OBRIŠI) or the exact business name and confirm permanent deletion.

By e-mail

Send a request from the address you sign in with to advisory@veloracare.io with the subject “Bukoteka – account deletion” and your business name. We handle requests without undue delay and within one month at the latest.

What is deleted

Schedule and appointments, clients and phone numbers, services and opening hours, days off, earlier saved versions, online settings, link and bookings, feedback, the link to your sign-in and the subscription record in Bukoteka. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What is kept

Only a technical trial record: the internal account identifier and the start and end of the 30-day trial, without e-mail, business name or client data. This prevents restarting the free trial by deleting and re-creating an account. Short-lived abuse-protection records (hashed IP addresses) are removed automatically after about 24 hours.

The sign-in record in Firebase Authentication (Google, Apple or e-mail) and any customer record in Stripe are removed within 30 days of account deletion.
