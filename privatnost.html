Privatnost.

Bukoteka je aplikacija za raspored termina salona i malih uslužnih obrta. Radi u pregledniku te kao aplikacija za Android i iOS. Ova obavijest vrijedi za sve tri i objašnjava tko obrađuje koje podatke, zašto, gdje se čuvaju i kako ih možeš izvesti ili obrisati.

Tko je odgovoran

Velora, obrt za poslovne, administrativne i pomoćne usluge, vl. Ivan Brezac
Kapelica 1A, Kapelica, Hrvatska
OIB: 41834755074

  • Podaci klijenata salona (imena, telefoni, termini, napomene, online rezervacije): salon je voditelj obrade, a Velora je izvršitelj obrade koji te podatke obrađuje samo da bi Bukoteka radila za taj salon.
  • Podaci računa vlasnika salona (prijava, e-mail, postavke računa, povratne informacije, zapis o probi i pretplati): Velora je voditelj obrade.

Ugovor o obradi podataka klijenata prema članku 28. Opće uredbe o zaštiti podataka (GDPR) sastavni je dio Uvjeta korištenja: Dodatak A. Saloni iz pilota imaju ga i u pisanom ugovoru o pilotu.

Koji se podaci obrađuju

  • Račun: korisnički identifikator i e-mail, način prijave (Google, Apple na iPhoneu ili e-mail i lozinka), potvrda e-mail adrese, datum zadnje prijave. Kod prijave e-mailom lozinku provjerava Firebase Authentication; baza Bukoteke lozinku ne vidi ni ne pohranjuje. Kod Apple prijave možeš sakriti svoj e-mail; tada vidimo samo Appleovu adresu za prosljeđivanje.
  • Salon: naziv, djelatnost, usluge i trajanja, radno vrijeme, odmori i neradni dani. Ako u salonu radi više osoba: ime svake osobe, njezino radno vrijeme i usluge koje radi.
  • Računi osoblja: ako vlasnik salona osobi pošalje poveznicu za prijavu, čuvamo vezu njezina računa (e-mail prijave) sa salonom. Osoba vidi raspored i klijente tog salona i mijenja samo svoje termine; ne vidi postavke, pretplatu ni druge salone. Vlasnik salona vidi s kojim je e-mailom osoba povezana i može ukinuti pristup.
  • Klijenti i termini koje salon unese: ime, telefon (neobavezno), usluga, datum i vrijeme, napomena te osoba iz salona koja pruža uslugu (ako salon vodi više osoba).
  • Online rezervacije, ako ih salon uključi: ime i telefon osobe koja rezervira, neobavezna napomena za salon (do 300 znakova), stanje rezervacije (na čekanju, potvrđena, prilagođena ili odbijena) s eventualnom porukom salona, prihvat pravila i tehnički sažetak (hash) privatne poveznice za upravljanje rezervacijom. Svatko tko dobije tu privatnu poveznicu može upravljati rezervacijom u okviru pravila salona.
  • Do deset ranijih spremljenih stanja rasporeda za povrat nakon pogreške.
  • Povratne informacije poslane kroz obrazac u aplikaciji.
  • Proba i pretplata: početak i kraj tridesetodnevne probe, status paketa i, ako je pretplata plaćena, oznaka kupca i razdoblje kod pružatelja plaćanja. Mobilne aplikacije ne nude kupnju.
  • Zaštita od zloupotrebe online rezervacija: hash IP adrese vezan uz salon i sat, briše se nakon približno 24 sata.

Imeniku telefona pristupa se samo ručnim odabirom kontakta na podržanom uređaju; .vcf datoteke obrađuju se na uređaju i prenosi se samo odabrana osoba, ne cijeli imenik.

Bukoteka nije namijenjena zdravstvenim ni drugim posebnim kategorijama podataka. U napomene ne upisuj takve podatke. Nema oglašavanja ni marketinške analitike.

Svrha i pravna osnova

Podaci računa obrađuju se radi pružanja usluge (ugovor) i zaštite od zloupotrebe (legitimni interes). Podaci klijenata obrađuju se po uputi salona, radi vođenja njegova rasporeda i online rezervacija. Salon je odgovoran za pravnu osnovu i obavještavanje svojih klijenata.

Gdje se podaci čuvaju

Prijavu (Google, Apple i e-mail) omogućuje Firebase Authentication (Google), koji u mobilnim aplikacijama radi i na uređaju; vrijede i Googleova pravila privatnosti. Aplikacija i baza rasporeda nalaze se na Cloudflareu (Workers i baza D1, smještena u EU); vrijede i Cloudflareova pravila privatnosti. Izvorni kod je na GitHubu; korisnička baza nije tamo. Pretplata se, kad je uključena, plaća na webu preko Stripea; kartične podatke obrađuje Stripe, a Bukoteka ih ne pohranjuje. Mobilne aplikacije Android i iOS samo prikazuju web aplikaciju u vlastitom okviru; ne sadrže oglase, analitiku ni praćenje.

Tko ima pristup

Svaki račun vidi samo vlastiti salon. Drugi saloni nemaju pristup. Vlasnik projekta čita povratne informacije; kao operator može pristupiti pohranjenim podacima radi podrške i održavanja, samo kad je to potrebno. Na javnoj poveznici za online rezervacije vide se naziv, usluge, kontakt i pravila salona, imena osoba koje primaju klijente (ako ih je više) te slobodni termini, ali ne i podaci drugih klijenata. Potvrde se ne šalju automatski e-mailom ni SMS-om.

Koliko dugo

Podaci se čuvaju dok je račun aktivan. Brisanjem računa odmah se uklanjaju iz baze aplikacije.

Nakon brisanja računa zadržava se samo tehnički zapis o iskorištenoj probi: interna oznaka računa te početak i kraj probe, bez e-maila, naziva salona i podataka klijenata. Svrha je spriječiti ponovno pokretanje besplatnih 30 dana brisanjem i ponovnim otvaranjem računa (legitimni interes).

Opcija Postavke → Obriši račun i podatke trajno briše raspored, klijente, usluge, postavke, ranija stanja, online postavke i rezervacije, povratne informacije i zapis o pretplati. Brisanje možeš zatražiti i e-poštom na advisory@veloracare.io. Zapis prijave u Firebaseu i eventualni zapis kupca u Stripeu uklanjaju se u roku od 30 dana od brisanja računa. Više: Brisanje računa.

Upiti s web stranice (obrazac i WhatsApp)

Kad nam se javiš preko obrasca na web stranici Bukoteke (upit se sprema u bazu Bukoteke na Cloudflareu u EU i stiže nam kao e-mail) ili porukom na WhatsApp, obrađujemo podatke koje nam sam pošalješ: ime, e-mail ili telefon i sadržaj poruke te, iz obrasca, naziv salona, djelatnost i grad. Svrha je odgovoriti na tvoj upit i dogovoriti probu Bukoteke. Pravna osnova je tvoja privola i radnje na tvoj zahtjev prije sklapanja ugovora. Za te podatke Velora je voditelj obrade. Čuvamo ih dok upit ne bude riješen, a najdulje 12 mjeseci. Na tvoj zahtjev obrisat ćemo ih i ranije: piši na advisory@veloracare.io. Za poruke poslane WhatsAppom vrijede i pravila privatnosti WhatsAppa. Web stranica ne koristi kolačiće, trackere ni analitiku.

Poslovne poruke salonima

Salonima koji posluju kao trgovačko društvo (d.o.o. ili j.d.o.o.) možemo poslati jednu kratku poruku o Bukoteki na poslovnu adresu e-pošte objavljenu u javnom Sudskom registru (sudreg-data.gov.hr), i najviše jedan podsjetnik. Pritom obrađujemo naziv društva, tu adresu, djelatnost i mjesto te bilješku o slanju i odgovoru. Pravna osnova je legitimni interes predstavljanja usluge tvrtkama. Na odgovor „odjava” ili poruku na bok@bukoteka.hr više se ne javljamo, a adresu zadržavamo samo na popisu za odjavu, da se ne bismo ponovno javili. Ostale podatke brišemo najkasnije 12 mjeseci nakon slanja. Obradi možeš prigovoriti u bilo kojem trenutku.

Tvoja prava

Imaš pravo na pristup, ispravak, brisanje, ograničenje obrade, prenosivost podataka i prigovor. Klijenti salona svoja prava ostvaruju prvenstveno kod salona; Velora salonu pomaže. Pritužbu možeš podnijeti Agenciji za zaštitu osobnih podataka (AZOP, azop.hr).

Privacy.

Bukoteka is an appointment scheduling app for salons and small service businesses. It runs in the browser and as an Android and iOS app. This notice covers all three and explains who processes which data, why, where it is stored and how you can export or delete it.

Who is responsible

Velora, obrt za poslovne, administrativne i pomoćne usluge, owner Ivan Brezac
Kapelica 1A, Kapelica, Croatia
OIB (Croatian tax number): 41834755074
Contact: advisory@veloracare.io

  • Data about the business's clients (names, phone numbers, appointments, notes, online bookings): the business is the controller and Velora is the processor, processing this data only to run Bukoteka for that business.
  • Business owner account data (sign-in, e-mail, account settings, feedback, trial and subscription records): Velora is the controller.

The data processing agreement for client data under Article 28 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is part of the Terms of Use: Annex A. Pilot businesses also have it in their written pilot agreement.

What data is processed

  • Account: user identifier and e-mail, sign-in method (Google, Apple on iPhone, or e-mail and password), e-mail verification status, date of last sign-in. With e-mail sign-in the password is checked by Firebase Authentication; the Bukoteka database never sees or stores it. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your e-mail; we then see only Apple's relay address.
  • Business: name, industry, services and durations, opening hours, breaks and days off. If several people work at the business: each person's name, working hours and the services they provide.
  • Staff accounts: if the business owner sends a person a sign-in link, we store the link between that person's account (sign-in e-mail) and the business. The person sees that business's schedule and clients and changes only their own appointments; they do not see settings, the subscription or other businesses. The business owner sees which e-mail the person is linked with and can revoke access.
  • Clients and appointments entered by the business: name, phone (optional), service, date and time, note, and the staff member providing the service (if the business has several people).
  • Online bookings, if the business enables them: name and phone of the person booking, an optional note for the business (up to 300 characters), the booking status (pending, confirmed, adjusted or declined) with any message from the business, acceptance of the rules and a technical hash of the private link used to manage the booking. Anyone who receives that private link can manage the booking within the business's rules.
  • Up to ten earlier saved versions of the schedule, for recovery after a mistake.
  • Feedback sent through the in-app form.
  • Trial and subscription: start and end of the 30-day trial, plan status and, if a subscription is paid, the customer reference and period at the payment provider. The mobile apps do not offer purchases.
  • Abuse protection for online booking: a hash of the IP address linked to the business and hour, removed after about 24 hours.

The phone's address book is accessed only when you manually pick a contact on a supported device; .vcf files are processed on the device and only the chosen person is transferred, never the whole address book.

Bukoteka is not intended for health data or other special categories of data. Do not enter such data in notes. There is no advertising or marketing analytics.

Purpose and legal basis

Account data is processed to provide the service (contract) and to prevent abuse (legitimate interest). Client data is processed on the business's instructions to run its schedule and online booking. The business is responsible for the legal basis and for informing its clients.

Where data is stored

Sign-in (Google, Apple and e-mail) is provided by Firebase Authentication (Google), which in the mobile apps also runs on the device; Google's privacy policy also applies. The app and the schedule database run on Cloudflare (Workers and a D1 database located in the EU); Cloudflare's privacy policy also applies. Source code is on GitHub; the user database is not. When a subscription is enabled it is paid on the web through Stripe; card data is processed by Stripe and Bukoteka does not store it. The Android and iOS apps only show the web app in their own frame; they contain no ads, analytics or tracking.

Who has access

Each account sees only its own business. Other businesses have no access. The project owner reads feedback; as operator, the owner can access stored data for support and maintenance only when needed. The public online booking link shows the business name, services, contact, rules, the names of the people taking clients (if there are several) and available times, but not other clients' data. Confirmations are not sent automatically by e-mail or SMS.

How long

Data is kept while the account is active. Deleting the account removes it from the app database immediately.

After an account is deleted, only a technical trial record is kept: the internal account identifier and the trial start and end, without e-mail, business name or client data. Its purpose is to prevent restarting the free 30 days by deleting and re-creating an account (legitimate interest).

Export, correction and deletion

In Settings you can download a backup (JSON) and correct data in the app. Settings → Delete account and data permanently deletes the schedule, clients, services, settings, earlier versions, online settings and bookings, feedback and the subscription record. You can also request deletion by e-mail at advisory@veloracare.io. The sign-in record in Firebase and any customer record in Stripe are removed within 30 days of account deletion. More: Account deletion.

Website inquiries (form and WhatsApp)

When you contact us through the form on the Bukoteka website (your inquiry is stored in the Bukoteka database on Cloudflare in the EU and reaches us by e-mail) or by WhatsApp message, we process the data you send us: your name, e-mail or phone and the content of your message and, from the form, your business name, type of business and town. The purpose is to reply to your inquiry and arrange a Bukoteka trial. The legal basis is your consent and steps taken at your request before entering into a contract. Velora is the controller for this data. We keep it until your inquiry is resolved, for no longer than 12 months. We will delete it earlier on request: write to advisory@veloracare.io. Messages sent via WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy. The website uses no cookies, trackers or analytics.

Business messages to salons

We may send salons that operate as a company (d.o.o. or j.d.o.o.) one short message about Bukoteka to the business email address published in the public Croatian court register (sudreg-data.gov.hr), and at most one reminder. For this we process the company name, that address, the type of business and town, and a note of the message and any reply. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in presenting the service to businesses. If you reply “odjava” (unsubscribe) or write to bok@bukoteka.hr, we will not contact you again and keep the address only on a do-not-contact list. Other data is deleted no later than 12 months after sending. You can object to this processing at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. The business's clients exercise their rights primarily with the business; Velora assists the business. You can lodge a complaint with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP, azop.hr).

Trial and subscription

The 30-day trial does not ask for a card and never turns into a paid subscription automatically.
